What affects the price

Why we price your job, not a rate card

Two houses on the same street can need very different scaffolds. These are the things that set the price, so it helps to tell us about them in the job sheet.

  • HeightHow many storeys, and whether we need to reach a chimney or ridge.
  • How much of the houseOne elevation, two sides, or all the way round.
  • Access and groundNarrow passages, conservatories, slopes, soft ground or a neighbour's side.
  • How long it stays upA few days for a gutter repair, or several weeks for a re-roof.
  • ExtrasA temporary roof, alarms and lighting, or a pavement licence.

Living with a scaffold

What it's like having one up

A scaffold changes your house for a few weeks. Here's what we look after, and what's worth thinking about before it goes up.

  • Safe for your family and the publicErected by CISRS-qualified scaffolders to NASC guidance, and inspected before anyone uses it.
  • Vetted people at your homeEvery member of staff and every sub-contractor is DBS checked.
  • Security while it's standingScaffold alarms and lighting are available if you're worried about access to upper windows.
  • Pavements and roadsIf the scaffold needs to stand on the pavement or road, the council's highways team must license it. Mention it when you ask for a quote.
  • Your neighboursIf the scaffold needs to go on or over a neighbour's side, it's worth talking to them early. We'll explain what's needed when we look at the job.

Questions

Domestic scaffolding FAQs

How much does domestic scaffolding cost?

It depends on the height, the length of the run, access and how long you need it, so every job is priced on its own. Send us the details or photos through the job sheet and we'll quote properly rather than guess.

Do I need a licence for scaffolding on the pavement?

If any part of the scaffold stands on a public pavement or road, a licence is needed from the council's highways team. Mention it when you ask for a quote and we'll go through what applies to your street.

Who uses the scaffold once it's up?

Your roofer, builder, painter or window fitter. We erect it, inspect it before handing it over, and come back to alter it if the work changes.

Do you work on listed buildings and new builds?

Yes. Our domestic work includes listed buildings and new builds as well as everyday repairs and maintenance.

Can you keep the rain off while my roof is open?

Yes. A temporary roof over the scaffold keeps the weather off while the roofing work goes on, and we've put them up on homes in Bedford.

Are your scaffolders vetted?

Our employees and contractors are CISRS qualified and DBS checked, and we follow NASC guidance on scaffold safety.
